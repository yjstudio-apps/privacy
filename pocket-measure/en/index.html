Privacy Policy — Pocket Measure

2026-10-04

This policy describes privacy practices for Pocket Measure by YJ Studio (Android: com.yjlab.pocketmeasure).

1. Measurements and local storage

Ruler ticks and conversions are computed on-device. Tilt values are processed in memory while the level/angle screen is active; measurement history is not stored. DataStore saves ruler scale, sensor zeros, tolerance/feedback, theme, favorite tool and last-used tool. Conversion inputs are screen state, not saved records. There is no backend, login or developer cloud sync.

2. Advertising and external services

The app uses Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) and User Messaging Platform. Release builds use app-specific production advertising IDs; development builds use Google demo IDs. Actual ad availability depends on Google app review and inventory. The SDK initializes and requests ads only when consent status permits requests; slots may stay empty when consent configuration cannot be established.

For advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, the SDK may collect/share device, ad and app-set identifiers, IP-based approximate location, app/ad interactions and performance diagnostics. SDK traffic is encrypted in transit and may be processed internationally. Google applies its retention/deletion rules. Measurements, conversion inputs, code queries and appliance names/models are not added to ad requests.

“Ad privacy choices” appears in settings when required. Android also lets you reset/delete your advertising ID, which does not stop every network or diagnostic process.

3. Permissions

Internet/network and ad ID are used by ad/consent SDKs. Vibration is for user-enabled level feedback. Tilt sensors run while a measuring screen is active. Camera, microphone, contacts, precise location and broad storage access are not requested. Card calibration uses no card numbers or photos.

4. Reset

“Reset settings and calibration” clears calibration, zeros, favorites and preferences. Measurements do not replace verified professional tools.

5. Retention, protection and deletion

Saved information remains in app-private device storage until deleted. There is no separate app-level encryption; use Android device protection such as a screen lock. App data is configured to be excluded from automatic cloud backup and device transfer. OS/manufacturer features remain subject to their provider policies.

Use Android Settings → Apps → this app → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall, to delete local data. In-app controls are described below. Local deletion does not erase Google's ad data or information already shared. We cannot remotely access local app data. You can edit/delete it on your device or contact us about rights under applicable law.

6. Contact, external links and children

If you email us, we process your email address and message for the time needed to answer and follow up, then delete unnecessary information unless legally required to retain it. Do not send card numbers, passwords or other sensitive information.

Opening official sources, policies or an email app applies the destination provider’s policy. Website visits may be handled under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The app is not designed for children under 14 and does not require registration.

7. Changes and contact

We update this page/date when functionality or processing changes and notify within the app when needed.
