Privacy Policy — WorkTalk Arabic

Effective: 4 October 2026 · Last updated: 4 October 2026

This policy describes privacy for WorkTalk Arabic by YJ Studio (Android package com.worktalk.urduarabic, version 1.0.0). The app provides offline Arabic learning for restaurant and kitchen work, with Urdu and English meanings.

1. Collection and sharing

This version has no registration, login, developer server, analytics or advertising SDK. The developer does not collect, transmit, share or sell personal information or learning records from the app.

2. Learning records on your device

Progress, quiz results and mistakes, review dates, favorites, streaks and language, audio speed, goal and reminder settings are stored only on your device. Learning and records work without internet access. Android cloud backup of app data is disabled.

3. Audio and device permissions

Arabic phrases, translations and pre-generated recordings are bundled with the app. Playback does not contact an online speech service. The app does not record your voice or request microphone, camera, contacts or location access. Vibration is used for optional quiz feedback.

4. Optional learning reminders

Notification permission is used to show learning reminders at your chosen local device time. Reminders may be restored after restarting the device. You can disable them in the app or Android settings. Reminder schedules are not sent to a server.

5. Retention, deletion and your controls

Local records remain on your device until you clear app data or uninstall the app. In Android Settings → Apps → WorkTalk Arabic → Storage → Clear data, you can delete records and settings. Deleted records cannot be restored. The developer cannot access your device learning records.

6. Children

The app is intended for adult learners working in restaurants and kitchens and is not directed to children under 13. It does not collect ages or personal information.

7. Contact and external websites

If you email a privacy inquiry, your email address and message are used to respond. Please do not send sensitive personal information. The app itself does not collect email contents. This policy page is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may process website visitor information under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

8. Changes

We update the last-updated date on this page when the policy changes. Before adding advertising or online features, we will update this policy and Google Play data safety information and provide any required notices and consent choices.
