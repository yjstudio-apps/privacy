Privacy Policy — WorkTalk Arabic

Effective: 4 October 2026 · Updated: 26 October 2026

WorkTalk Arabic stores learning progress, assessment answers, mistakes, favorites and settings on your device. We do not send these learning records to a developer server. There is no login or developer analytics service. Android cloud backup is disabled.

Version 1.5.0 includes Google AdMob banner ads on the home screen. We request non-personalized ads. When ads connect to the internet, Google's advertising SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate approximate location), device identifiers including advertising/app set IDs, ad and app interactions, and diagnostics for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Non-personalized ads can still process this data. SDK data is encrypted in transit. Google's Privacy Policy and advertising policies apply.

Google's consent form is shown where required before ad requests are allowed. When available, open My → Ad privacy choices to review your choice. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings. Ads may be unavailable without a network or valid privacy choice; learning remains available.

Arabic text, translations and audio are included in the app. Learning and audio work offline. We do not record your voice or request microphone, camera, contacts or precise location permissions.

Optional notifications remind you to learn at your chosen local time. You can turn them off in the app or Android settings. Clear app storage or uninstall to delete local learning records; deleted records cannot be restored. Google handles advertising data under its own retention and privacy controls.

The app is intended for adult restaurant and kitchen workers, not children. The online policy provides Google's privacy and advertising links. Email inquiries are used to respond to you.
