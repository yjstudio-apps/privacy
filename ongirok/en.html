Privacy Policy — HeartLedger

Effective and last updated: October 23, 2026

YJ Studio (Google Play developer name: YJ Lab) provides HeartLedger / 마음장부, Android package com.ongirok.app. This policy describes the journal and advertising updates, including versions 1.2.0 and later and 1.3.0. Advertising-related processing does not apply to older versions without advertising.

1. Records stored on your device

People’s names, nicknames, relationships, groups and notes; event types, titles, dates, places and notes; money amounts, direction, names, delivery details and completion status; attendance; gift names, quantities, gold weights, units, dates and notes; and app settings are stored in the app’s local storage.

Journal records are processed on your device for entry, display, search, totals, editing, deletion and backups you request. We do not send journal records to a developer server or advertising service. We cannot remotely access your local journal. The demo uses fictional records in separate storage, and search does not send records to an external AI service.

2. Advertising services

There is no app account, developer-operated journal server, separate usage analytics tool or automatic cloud sync. Advertising versions use Google AdMob (GMA Next-Gen SDK) and UMP. For advertising, analytics and fraud prevention, Google’s advertising SDK may collect and share:

  • Approximate location inferred from an IP address, without requesting precise GPS location.
  • App and ad interactions, such as launches, impressions and taps.
  • App and SDK performance diagnostics.
  • Device or other identifiers, such as advertising and app-set IDs.

SDK data is encrypted in transit with TLS. Google’s handling, retention, deletion and advertising partners are described in its advertising information and privacy policy. We do not keep a separate copy of this advertising data in a journal server.

3. Advertising privacy choices

Where supported and required, UMP provides privacy choices. When an entry point is required, open Settings → Ads and privacy → Ad privacy settings in the app to review your choices. Available choices and advertising depend on your region, choice status and Google’s policies. Declining personalized advertising may still allow basic processing for non-personalized or limited ads.

Journal features remain available if consent checks or ads fail. We do not request ads without a valid status allowing an ad request. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in Android’s advertising privacy settings; this does not stop every type of advertising data processing.

4. Backups and imports you choose

Exports save JSON or CSV through Android’s system file picker to a location you choose. If you choose cloud storage or another external service, that provider receives the file and its policy applies. Backup files are not sent to the developer. Imports read a file you choose, show a preview and save after confirmation. Use a JSON backup to preserve and restore the full journal relationships.

Backup files are not encrypted and may contain personal and money records. Store them in a trusted location and share only what is needed.

5. Permissions

Advertising and privacy checks use internet and network-state permissions; the advertising SDK includes the advertising ID permission. Journal entry, search and viewing work offline. We do not request contacts, precise location, camera, full photo-library or broad file-storage access. Backups and imports only access the file or location selected in the system picker.

6. Retention and deletion

Local records and settings remain until you delete them or remove app data. Deleting an event removes its linked money, attendance and gifts. Clear all local records through Android Settings → Apps → HeartLedger → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. Labels may vary by device.

Clearing app data does not remotely delete advertising data already processed by Google; use Google’s information and controls for that data. Exported files, copies shared with other apps and cloud copies remain separate and must be deleted at their respective locations. Back up needed records before uninstalling or changing devices.

7. Protection and access

Records use app-private storage and operating-system access controls. The app does not provide its own encrypted storage, encrypted backup or app-lock feature. Use your device lock, system updates and secure backup storage to protect records.

You can view, correct and delete records in the app and export a full JSON backup. There is no server account to delete. Contact us below about applicable privacy requests; we cannot remotely read or alter your device’s journal.

Journal records are configured to be excluded from Android automatic cloud backup and automatic device transfer. Operating-system and device-maker features are subject to their own policies and settings.

8. Contact, website and children

If you email us, we process the email address and message you provide to answer and follow up, and remove unneeded information when that purpose ends unless retention is legally required. Do not send passwords, financial account details, third-party records or a complete journal backup unless needed.

This website is hosted on GitHub Pages; website access information may be processed under the GitHub privacy statement. External email and storage providers have their own policies. HeartLedger is designed for adults managing personal events, not for children.

9. Updates and developer contact

When features or processing change, we update this page and its revision date and provide an app update or notice where needed.
